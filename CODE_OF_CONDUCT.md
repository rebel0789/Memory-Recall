# Code of Conduct

We use the Contributor Covenant 2.1 principles: a harassment-free, inclusive, respectful community; constructive feedback; transparent enforcement; and protection against retaliation.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discriminatory language, unwanted sexual attention, doxxing, deliberate disruption, or publishing private information without permission.

Maintainers may correct, warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently remove participants according to severity and pattern. Use the private channels in `MAINTAINERS.md` for conduct reports involving private information.

The complete Contributor Covenant 2.1 is available at <https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>.
