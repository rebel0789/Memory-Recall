# Licensing Strategy

The core, SDKs, schemas, and first-party adapters use Apache-2.0 unless a reviewed exception states otherwise. The patent grant supports broad community and commercial adoption.

Optional upstream projects remain separate adapters or services. A repository being publicly visible or having an open client does not imply its full backend is open source. License is re-verified at the exact pinned commit.

Strong-copyleft, source-available, or commercial components must remain clearly bounded and must not be copied into the Apache core without deliberate legal review. This document is an engineering policy, not legal advice.
